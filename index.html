07 FAQ

Before
you flash.

Straight answers, no mystery meat. If something is missing, the Telegram group is one tap away.

Ask the community ↗
Which file should I flash?

Use the download matcher above. HyperOS users choose the HyperOS build; AOSP and custom ROM users choose AOSP. Then pick KowSU + SuSFS, clean KernelSU, or No Root. Always flash the AnyKernel3 ZIP — not a raw image or DTB.

Will this work on another Xiaomi device?

No. This kernel is built specifically for the POCO F4 and Redmi K40S (munch / munch-in) with SM8250-AC. Flashing across devices can permanently break boot or worse.

Does flashing wipe my data?

The kernel installer does not wipe user data. Still, a bad flash or mismatched package can leave you recovering. Back up boot and vendor_boot first, and know how to restore them.

What is the difference between the root flavors?

KowSU + SuSFS includes kernel-level stealth with SuSFS v2.3.0 and is recommended for most rooted users. Clean KernelSU keeps standard root without SuSFS hooks. No Root is a pure kernel for banking, stock-like use, or anyone who does not want root.

Which ROM versions are supported?

HyperOS 1.0–4.0 (Android 14–17) and AOSP-based ROMs from Android 13–17. The AOSP build has been tested on Android 15, 16 and 17 with ROMs such as LineageOS, crDroid, Evolution X and PixelOS.

How do I control the kernel profiles?

Write 0, 1 or 2 to /sys/kernel/extreme_profile, or use the names gaming, balanced and battery. The Extreme Kernel Manager app gives you a one-tap dashboard and live telemetry.

Why does my Play Integrity check still fail?

Kernel-side SuSFS stealth is only part of the picture. Integrity verdicts also depend on your ROM, root manager configuration, Play Integrity Fix setup and device certification. Use the support group to debug your exact combination.
